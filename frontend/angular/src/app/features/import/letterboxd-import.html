Brings your diary and watchlist over from Letterboxd.

  1. On letterboxd.com, open Settings, then Data, and choose Export your data.
  2. Upload the ZIP archive you get, without unpacking it.

Imported are the diary (dates, ratings, rewatches, reviews, likes) and the watchlist. The export has no TMDB IDs, so every film is looked up on TMDB by title and year; films that TMDB does not know become manual movies. The other files of the archive, such as your profile, are neither read nor stored. Importing the same export again is safe: what exists already is skipped.
